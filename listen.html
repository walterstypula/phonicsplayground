Listening check

Every recording the games use, so they can be heard on its own. Nothing here is part of the game — it is a bench for checking what a sound actually sounds like.

If you are helping by listening: click a sound and say whether it is the sound you would make yourself when reading that word to a child.

The word under each one is where that sound is heard. Under b it says "bat", so it should sound like the beginning of bat — a short push of air, not "buh" as a whole word. A couple say "the end sound" instead, because that is where those live: ng is the end of ring.

The ones worth flagging are: a sound that has turned into a different sound, a sound with an "uh" stuck on the end, and anything too faint or too clipped to make out.
